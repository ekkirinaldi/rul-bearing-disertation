Executive summary

This report consolidates 1 RUL experiment run(s) into one dissertation-style artefact. It combines aggregate metrics, model-level diagnostics, train/validation dynamics, held-out bearing predictions, residual analysis, execution logs, and full merged configuration for reproducibility.

  • Rank models by test behavior, not validation alone: validation RMSE selects checkpoints, but held-out bearing plots show whether that checkpoint generalizes.
  • Use multiple metrics: RMSE emphasizes large mistakes, MAE shows typical absolute error, R² shows explained variance, and PHM score captures the asymmetric cost profile used in bearing prognostics.
  • Check runtime and parameters: a lower RMSE is only compelling if its training time and model size are acceptable for the intended research comparison.
