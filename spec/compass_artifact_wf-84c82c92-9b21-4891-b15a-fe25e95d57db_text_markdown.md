# Charting a novel dissertation on bearing RUL prediction

**The strongest publishable dissertation angle for 2026 combines selective state-space models (Mamba) with mechanistic interpretability and distribution-free uncertainty, evaluated bidirectionally on PHM2012 and XJTU-SY.** This report maps the current SOTA, documents where the field has saturated, identifies five genuinely underexplored intersections, and recommends concrete methodological paths with target venues. The bearing RUL literature grew explosively in 2023–2025 (dominated by Transformer hybrids and domain-adaptation variants), but most gains are incremental percentages on inconsistent benchmarks; foundation models, calibrated UQ under domain shift, mechanistic interpretability (SAE-style), and physics-guided diffusion augmentation remain largely untouched on the two canonical bearing datasets. Because PHM2012 has only 17 run-to-failure trajectories and XJTU-SY only 15, the field's central unsolved problem is **generalisation from scarce, non-stationary, sometimes non-monotonic data** — the axis where novelty should be concentrated.

---

## 1. The SOTA landscape has bifurcated into Transformer hybrids and emerging SSMs

The dominant architectural family on PHM2012 and XJTU-SY in 2023–2025 is the **Transformer-hybrid**: CNN/TCN/LSTM front-ends that extract local vibration features, fed into attention-based sequence models. MDSCT (depth-wise separable convolutions + ProbSparse attention, Heliyon 2024) reports RMSE improvements of 0.7–10.1 percentage points over prior baselines on PHM2012 and 1.5–10.8 pp on XJTU-SY. The Cross-Transformer with segmented cleaning (RESS 2024) achieves RMSE 1.88 under constant conditions on PHM2012 and a 59.1% reduction under variable conditions. EMD + Transformer-BiLSTM (Applied Sciences 2025) reports normalised RMSE 0.0563/MAE 0.0469 on PHM2012 and 0.0442/0.0374 on XJTU-SY — among the lowest published numbers, though normalisation conventions differ. Pathformer-TCLSTM with TSDAE pre-training (IJPHM 2025) and the TCN–Transformer hybrid (Sensors 2025, –39.67% RMSE vs TCN) represent the current plateau of that design space.

**State-space models are the clearest architectural frontier.** Mamba-SDP (Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology 2025) is the lone paper reporting full metrics on both datasets using a Mamba backbone with scaled-dot-product channel fusion: **RMSE 11.50/MAE 8.99/Score 0.82 on PHM2012 and RMSE 11.40/MAE 9.20/Score 0.81 on XJTU-SY** (absolute units). The xLSTM–Transformer (Frontiers/PMC 2026) targets cross-condition generalisation on PHM2012 with variable-condition ablations. **Fewer than five bearing-RUL papers use Mamba as of early 2026**, which is a strong signal of publishability.

Other architectural families remain niche on these two datasets. GNNs (SAGCN + Self-Attention, MSSP 2023; the GNN-RUL benchmark at github.com/Frank-Wang-oss/GNN_RUL_Benchmarking) mostly benchmark on C-MAPSS; SAGCN-SA reports ~0.170 average RMSE on XJTU-SY. Diffusion models appear almost exclusively as **augmentation** (Defect-Guided Conditional Diffusion, ASCE J. Risk 2025; CFGDMHD in RESS 2024; DiffRUL in RESS 2024) rather than as the RUL regressor itself — a gap Angle E below exploits. Physics-informed networks are the third emerging family: DSCN-AttnPINN (JAMST 2024), PI-MSTFN (RESS 2024), BearingNet (MSSP 2025), and the Paris-law PINN with Gaussian processes (Integrating Materials and Manufacturing Innovation 2026) demonstrate that physics residuals can match data-driven accuracy while improving interpretability. Self-supervised/contrastive methods (HNCPM 2023, DCSSL Sci. Rep. 2026, SSPCL RESS 2022, TF-C NeurIPS 2022) and foundation-model approaches (LM4RUL arXiv 2501.07191, Pulse SSRN 2025, FR-LLM RESS 2025, BearLLM 2024) are immature and almost entirely based on chat-style LLMs rather than modern time-series foundation models (Chronos, TimesFM, MOIRAI-2).

The PHM-GPT paper (Engineering 2025) introduces an InsPHM-456k dataset for foundation-model pre-training.

## 2. Transfer learning is saturated on marginal alignment but open on regression-specific and cross-dataset problems

Domain adaptation for bearing RUL is the most crowded sub-area of the literature. The mature techniques — DANN, Deep CORAL, MMD/MK-MMD, JMMD — have been applied extensively. Representative papers include BGRU-DANN (PeerJ CS 2021) on PHM2012 cross-condition; ADACNN (Sci. Rep. 2022) with explicit cross-platform PHM2012↔IMS; TBiLSTM + MK-MMD (Rathore & Harsha, Eng. Failure Analysis 2022); Wasserstein-weighted DA (Hu et al., RESS 224, 2022) on XJTU-SY; and **JDATransformer (EAAI 2025)**, which is the leading recent example of cross-machine transfer explicitly between PHM2012 and XJTU-SY using Joint-MMD inside Transformer attention (reporting ΔRMSE –0.2128 intra-dataset, –0.0626 cross-dataset).

**Subdomain adaptation is where the most recent innovation lives.** DSAN-WM (KBS 2024) uses multi-source weighted LMMD with degradation-stage-based subdomain alignment across eight PHM2012+XJTU-SY migration tasks. HIWSAN (Sensors 2025) uses HI-weighted LMMD. SC-SAN (Sensors 2025) introduces dynamic spectral-clustering subdomain boundaries. SDA-CIC (J. Manufacturing Systems 2025) adds contextual-information compensation with per-epoch subdomain repartitioning. **ERCDAN (RESS 2024)** is the most credible cross-machine reference, combining CBAM with attenuation MK-MMD across PHM2012, XJTU-SY, and EBFL to mitigate negative transfer.

Few-shot and meta-learning are less mature. MAML-based approaches (Ding et al., JIM 2022; MetaDFKN, RESS 2024) and **Bayesian MAML with predictive uncertainty calibration (BMLPUC, EAAI 2024)** have been validated on PHM2012+XJTU-SY. Meta-SGD for bearings (Structural Health Monitoring 2025) addresses variable conditions. Self-supervised pre-training is represented by SSPCL (RESS 2022), HNCPM (2023), and **DCSSL (Sci. Rep. 2026)**, a dual-dimensional contrastive SSL method validated on both datasets. Source-free DA is an emerging third wave: **CURA (MSSP 2025)** uses evidential-learning-based source-free adaptation for incomplete target data; UE-SFDA (IEEE TII 2024) does self-supervised knowledge distillation; Wu et al. (RESS 2024) generalise regression SFDA to rotating machinery.

**Genuine transfer-learning gaps remain in four places.** First, **true test-time adaptation** (TENT/EATA-style single-pass, label-free inference-time updates) is virtually absent for RUL — nearly all "online" work batch-fine-tunes or assumes some target labels. Second, **symmetric bidirectional PHM2012↔XJTU-SY transfer** reported under one protocol with one model remains rare despite ERCDAN, JDATransformer, and Dynamic Hybrid DA (KBS 2024) addressing cross-machine. Third, **regression pseudo-label calibration** under source-free constraints is open (CURA partially addresses it, but only for incomplete target trajectories). Fourth, **domain generalisation without any target access** — the realistic deployment scenario — is only touched by AOA (KBS 2022) and ASC_DG (Measurement 2025), neither of which handles cross-machine shift.

## 3. Interpretability and uncertainty are well-motivated but poorly integrated

XAI for bearing RUL is currently dominated by three approaches. **Post-hoc feature attribution** (SHAP, LIME) appears in CARLE (Soft Computing 2025) — a Res-CNN + Res-LSTM + multi-head-attention + Random Forest hybrid with both LIME and SHAP explanations on PRONOSTIA+XJTU-SY — and the IDFC + LSTM + SHAP framework (Springer 2025). **Attention visualisation** is the default interpretability story in most Transformer papers (DTADAN, IEEE TIM 2023; MDSCT, Heliyon 2024; Conditional Variational Transformer, AEI 2024; Dynamic Temporal Attention + Mixed MLP, Autonomous Intelligent Systems 2024) — but attention heatmaps are empirically unreliable as explanations, so this remains weak evidence. **Physics-guided interpretability** is the most principled direction: DcRNN (JDMD 2022) embeds monotonic degradation physics; PI-MSTFN (RESS 2024) uses an adaptive I-DFT; DSCN-AttnPINN (JAMST 2024) combines separable convolutions with PINN; BearingNet (MSSP 2025) is a signal-processing-informed PINN; STA-HPINN (arXiv 2024) uses hidden-physics with spatiotemporal attention; and the AE + PINN ball-bearing paper (SHM 2025) uses acoustic-emission-driven diffusion-entropy HI.

Uncertainty quantification has matured more visibly. Bayesian approaches include **BLKAN (RESS 2023)** with large-kernel Bayesian convolution, two-stage Bayesian DL (Computers & Electrical Engineering 2023), and Bayesian NN with CNN-LSTM backbone (QREI 2023). Monte Carlo Dropout appears in the LSTM+KDE framework (Entropy 2022) on PHM2012 Bearing 1_3. **Conformal prediction is the most exciting recent thread**: Javanmardi & Hüllermeier (IJPHM 2023, arXiv 2212.14612) introduced CP intervals for RUL on C-MAPSS; Jean-Pierre et al. (IJPHM 2025) combined LSTM/Transformer + CP with censoring; CoPAL (PMLR 2024) uses CP-driven active learning; a 2025 Future Internet paper builds a robust CP framework for IoT predictive maintenance; and **RobustUQ (RESS 262, 2025)** combines diffusion imputation with conformal prediction and metric learning. Evidential DL (**DET-MCD, Computers & Industrial Engineering 2026**) jointly models aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty. Quantile regression with subdomain adaptation (**DSATQRN, ESWA 2024**) and variational Bayesian envelope-spectrum models (**CVAER, Sensors 2024**) round out the probabilistic side.

**The key unfilled gap is the integration of UQ with domain shift.** Conformal prediction assumes exchangeability, which breaks under cross-condition or cross-machine transfer — yet no published bearing-RUL paper properly combines unsupervised domain adaptation with non-exchangeable conformal prediction. A **2025 benchmark on UQ for deep learning prognostics (RESS 253)** benchmarks BNN, MCD, Deep Ensembles, and heteroscedastic NN on N-CMAPSS but not on PHM2012/XJTU-SY. **Mechanistic interpretability** — sparse autoencoders probing internal representations, as Anthropic-style methods do for LLMs — has never been applied to bearing prognostics, though early sparse-AE work on Chronos (arXiv preprint 2025) shows feasibility.

## 4. Twelve research gaps that are genuinely open in 2025–2026

**Early-stage RUL and First Prediction Time detection** remain heuristic (3σ of RMS/kurtosis). Recent improvements include adaptive cross-validation FPT (Tandfonline NDT&E 2025), RRDT + DCDTN (Digital Signal Processing 2025), and transfer Fuzzy-C-Means (IJMLC 2025), but **no end-to-end learnable FPT jointly optimised with the RUL regressor exists** for either dataset. **Variable operating conditions** are the most-attacked gap (JDATransformer, ERCDAN, DHDA+A-CL, xLSTM-Transformer), but concurrent load + speed + bearing-geometry shift remains unsolved and negative transfer persists. **Noise robustness** is addressed mostly by pre-denoising (VMD, wavelet scattering); no SNR-conditioned adversarial benchmark exists.

**Limited run-to-failure data** is the field's defining constraint — 32 total trajectories across both datasets. Diffusion augmentation is proven for fault diagnosis (CFGDMHD, Defect-Guided Conditional Diffusion, DiffRUL) but **not yet applied to full-life-cycle RUL trajectory generation**. **Multi-sensor fusion** is conspicuously underused: PHM2012's temperature channel is routinely ignored, and no public cross-modal benchmark with horizontal+vertical+temperature exists on either dataset. **Non-monotonic degradation and self-healing** (lube-induced spall rounding, confirmed in Sensors 2025 ASH-HI paper and Applied Acoustics 2024 mechanism-model paper) are silently smoothed out by most pipelines; no benchmark reports error *inside* the healing window.

**Benchmark reproducibility** is a field-level crisis documented in the 2025 MSSP evaluation framework — there are no canonical train/val/test splits for PHM2012 or XJTU-SY, and papers silently choose normalisation that makes numbers incomparable. **Edge deployment** works only for *diagnosis* (ESP32-S3 at 88.3% accuracy, 45 ms, 17.7 mJ; GLSVLSI 2025 1D-CNN >99%); edge RUL *regression* with INT8/pruned models is unpublished. **Foundation models** for bearing RUL are the single most underexploited direction — LM4RUL, Pulse, FR-LLM, and BearLLM are all chat-LLM-based; **no Chronos/TimesFM/MOIRAI paper on PHM2012/XJTU-SY exists** as of early 2026. **Cross-dataset bidirectional transfer** reported under one protocol with one model is rare. **Continual learning** with Fisher-informed or gradient-projection methods (RESS 2025) has barely touched bearing RUL; catastrophic forgetting studies on PHM2012/XJTU-SY are nearly absent.

## 5. Five publishable dissertation angles ranked by novelty and feasibility

The following five directions sit at underexploited intersections of RUL + transfer + explainability + uncertainty. They are ordered by estimated publishability × feasibility on PHM2012 and XJTU-SY.

### Angle A — Interpretable selective state-space models (Mamba) for bearing RUL

The problem is that Transformer hybrids have saturated and remain opaque. The proposed method is a **bidirectional selective SSM** (Mamba-2/Mamba-3 backbone) with three novel components: spline-encoded time tokens (ss-Mamba style) providing interpretable temporal basis; Δ-gate-score channel pruning for edge deployability; and **sparse-autoencoder probing of intermediate states** to produce "degradation concept features" mapped to physical regimes (healthy / thinning lube / spall initiation / accelerated failure). This extends Mamba-SDP (JMST 2025) and SiMBA-PINN (Machines 2025) by adding *causally-validated* feature ablation that has never been done for bearings. **Feasibility is high** — Mamba trains in hours on a single GPU, 32 trajectories suffice for 10M-parameter models with pre-training across both datasets. Target venue: **MSSP**, which is riding the Mamba/SSM wave (PIMSTFN 2024, BearingNet 2025). Main challenges are SAE probe reliability under small-sample regimes and the reviewer demand to benchmark against every Transformer variant.

### Angle B — Conformal prediction + unsupervised domain adaptation for calibrated cross-condition RUL

The problem is that bearing RUL models increasingly drive safety decisions but lack distribution-free coverage guarantees under operating-condition shift, where exchangeability breaks. The method combines adversarial/MMD-based UDA on vibration features with **weighted/adaptive conformal prediction handling non-exchangeability** (following the Barber et al. non-exchangeable CP framework and RobustUQ RESS 2025), plus online recalibration using a rolling calibration set as target-domain data streams in. Evaluation is symmetric PHM2012↔XJTU-SY with guaranteed (1−α) coverage even under drift. **This is genuinely novel**: all published bearing-RUL CP work is in-distribution, and all CP-for-RUL work is on C-MAPSS. Target venue: **RESS**, which published RobustUQ and hosts the non-exchangeability conversation. Feasibility is high because CP is post-hoc and model-agnostic; the main risk is that theoretical validity degrades under large domain gaps, requiring careful calibration set design that excludes training bearings.

### Angle C — Time-series foundation models + few-shot cross-dataset bearing RUL

With only 32 trajectories, in-domain training is structurally data-starved. The question: can **Chronos-2 (Oct 2025 release), TimesFM-2.0-500M, or MOIRAI-2** transfer zero-shot or few-shot to bearing RUL? The method converts vibration-derived health indicators (RMS, kurtosis, envelope-spectrum bands) into Chronos-tokenised sequences; performs zero-shot HI-trajectory forecasting with threshold-crossing RUL extraction; applies few-shot LoRA/adapter fine-tuning on PHM2012 tested on XJTU-SY (and symmetric); probabilistic forecasting via Chronos native sampling or conformal wrapping; and sparse-AE interpretability probing to identify bearing-specific "change-detection features". **No peer-reviewed Chronos/TimesFM/MOIRAI paper on bearing RUL exists** — the only foundation-model work is chat-LLM-based (LM4RUL, Pulse, FR-LLM, BearLLM). Target venue: **IEEE TII** (or MSSP if strongly methodological). Feasibility is medium-high — Chronos-bolt runs 300+ forecasts/s, 500M TimesFM fits on A100 — but the pre-training domain gap (kHz vibration vs hourly energy) is the dominant risk, making HI-level aggregation essential. Licensing matters (TimesFM non-commercial, Chronos Apache).

### Angle D — Physics-informed neural networks with joint epistemic and aleatoric UQ

Pure data-driven bearing RUL produces physically inconsistent trajectories (non-monotonic dips during healing, negative RUL) and point estimates lack calibrated uncertainty. The method extends PI-MSTFN (Yang et al., RESS 2024) and DcRNN by embedding Paris crack-growth and Hertzian contact fatigue residuals as soft constraints; adding a **monotonicity regulariser on latent HI driven by PHM2012's temperature channel** (currently ignored in the literature); epistemic UQ via Bayesian PINN with ensemble Kalman inversion (BPINN, ICPHM 2025); aleatoric UQ via MC-dropout + conformal wrapper (Transformer+MCD+CP in EAAI 2025); and evaluation on bearings that exhibit self-healing (XJTU-SY 1_3, PHM2012 1_4) with a dedicated **healing-window error metric**. The novelty is the joint PINN+BPINN+CP ablation with healing-aware metrics — no existing paper closes that loop. Target venue: **MSSP** (physics-rooted) or **RESS** (UQ-rooted). Main challenges: differentiable bearing-degradation PDEs are hard (most models are empirical exponential), physics-residual weights notoriously dominate loss, and reviewers will demand ablation of each physics term.

### Angle E — Physics-guided diffusion augmentation for cross-dataset bearing RUL

GAN-based synthesis suffers mode collapse and diffusion is proven for *fault-diagnosis* augmentation but under-applied to *RUL regression trajectories*. The method trains a **degradation-trend-conditional diffusion model** (DiffRUL-style) on PHM2012 to generate synthetic full-life vibration envelopes conditioned on (load, speed, FPT, EOL); applies physics-guided sampling using a bearing 2-DoF dynamic model as prior (Defect-Guided Conditional Diffusion, ASCE 2025); uses synthetic bearings to pre-train a transfer model then fine-tunes on sparse XJTU-SY targets; evaluates bidirectionally and reports distributional diversity (FID on envelope spectra). The novelty is *physics-guided full-life-cycle diffusion* + *bidirectional cross-dataset transfer* — CFGDMHD and Defect-Guided Conditional Diffusion address diagnosis, not full trajectories. Target venue: **Measurement** or **IEEE TII**. Feasibility is medium — full-life-cycle diffusion at 25.6 kHz is compute-intensive (each trajectory = 1–4 hours of vibration), so envelope/HI-space operation is essential. Main challenges: generating physically plausible healing patterns without collapse, avoiding leakage from real labels, and strong real-data ablation demands from reviewers.

### Ranking for dissertation selection

Angle A (Mamba + interpretability) has the best novelty-to-risk profile for a single-author dissertation because SSMs are newly legitimated on these datasets and SAE-based mechanistic interpretability is virgin territory for prognostics. Angle B (CP + UDA) is the most theoretically crisp and will likely publish fastest because CP machinery is plug-and-play. Angle C (foundation models) has the highest upside but the highest domain-gap risk; recommended only if the candidate has strong HI-engineering intuition. Angle D (PINN + joint UQ) is solid but crowded; it needs the healing-window evaluation to differentiate. Angle E (diffusion + transfer) is compute-heavy and most likely to encounter reviewer skepticism about synthetic data leakage, but has the clearest industrial story.

**A credible hybrid dissertation** combines A (the architectural contribution — interpretable Mamba) with B (the UQ contribution — conformal prediction under domain shift) evaluated bidirectionally on PHM2012 and XJTU-SY. This delivers three papers: an MSSP paper on interpretable Mamba, a RESS paper on conformal-DA, and a Measurement/TII paper on the unified benchmark with healing-aware metrics.

## 6. Publication strategy and venue calibration

**Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing (MSSP)** is the prestige target (impact ~8) with strong 2024–25 volume on physics-guided degradation, multi-state temporal-frequency networks, and signal-processing-informed explainable PINNs. It favours Angles A and D. **Reliability Engineering & System Safety (RESS, impact ~9)** publishes most of the serious UQ, transfer-learning, and continual-learning bearing work (RobustUQ, CURA, ERCDAN, MKWJDAN, Fisher-informed CL) and is the obvious home for Angle B. **IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII, impact ~11)** rewards system-level, deployable AI; it fits Angles C and E best. **Measurement (impact ~5)** is the friendliest Q1 venue for strong-experiment papers and suits Angle E well. **IJPHM** (open access, PHM Society) is the best venue for benchmark/standardisation contributions. **EAAI** has been a willing home for Transformer+MCD+CP and JDATransformer-style papers. For a dissertation targeting three publications plus a thesis monograph, the optimal portfolio is one MSSP paper (architecture/physics), one RESS paper (UQ/transfer), and one Measurement or TII paper (applied/benchmark).

## Conclusion

Bearing RUL prediction in 2026 is architecturally mature but epistemically immature. The field knows how to build accurate point estimators on PHM2012 and XJTU-SY — Transformer hybrids, Mamba, GNNs, and PINNs all work — but it cannot yet deliver **calibrated, interpretable, transferable** predictions under the realistic constraint of fewer than twenty run-to-failure trajectories per dataset. The most publishable PhD dissertation in this space will not compete for another 0.5 pp of RMSE; it will **close the loop between architecture, uncertainty, and physical interpretability under domain shift**. Selective SSMs with mechanistic interpretability, non-exchangeable conformal prediction under unsupervised domain adaptation, and modern time-series foundation models are the three specific intersections where novelty is genuine and demonstrable on the two canonical datasets. Everything else is variation on a saturated theme.
